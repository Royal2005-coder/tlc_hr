# tlc_hr

TLC HRM on Frappe HR v16. Everything TLC-specific lives here; frappe, erpnext, hrms and erpnextvn stay unmodified.

- Roles, role profiles and user permissions per TLC level (worker, shift lead and head, HR officer, HR lead, payroll, director).
- Approval workflows from the TLC routing table (nobody approves their own request, reason to reject, dated delegation).
- Vietnamese rules on top of erpnextvn: PIT in 5 brackets (Luật 109/2025), base salary and deductions of 2026.
- The `/tlc` portal: one home per role with the approvals inbox.
- The TLC demo seed (company, sites, departments, people, users).
